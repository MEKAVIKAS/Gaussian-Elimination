### Primary Mathematical Sources

1. **Strang, Gilbert.** *Introduction to Linear Algebra*. 5th ed., Wellesley-Cambridge Press, 2016.  
   - *Key Sections:* Chapter 2: "Solving Linear Equations" (Sections 2.1–2.3: Elimination using Elementary Matrices, Matrix Multiplication, and Inverses). Provides the theoretical backbone for elementary row matrices, pivoting strategies, and triangular factorization.

2. **Lay, David C., Steven R. Lay, and Judi J. McDonald.** *Linear Algebra and Its Applications*. 5th ed., Pearson, 2016.  
   - *Key Sections:* Chapter 1: "Linear Equations in Linear Algebra" (Sections 1.1–1.2: Systems of Linear Equations, Row Reduction, and Echelon Forms). Standardizes formal definitions for pivots, basic variables, echelon vs. reduced echelon forms, and the uniqueness theorem of row equivalence.

3. **Sanderson, Grant (3Blue1Brown).** *Essence of Linear Algebra*. 3Blue1Brown Educational Series, 2016.  
   - *Key Sections:* Chapters 2, 7, and 8: Visual geometric intuition of systems of equations as intersecting hyperplanes in $\mathbb{R}^3$, linear transformations of coordinate grids, and inverse matrix operations.

4. **Anton, Howard, and Chris Rorres.** *Elementary Linear Algebra: Applications Version*. 11th ed., John Wiley & Sons, 2014.  
   - *Key Sections:* Chapter 1: Gaussian elimination algorithms, row interchange mechanics, and backward substitution procedures.
